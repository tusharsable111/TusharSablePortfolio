## How I Lead

```text
Strategy and outcomes
        ↓
Roadmap and prioritization
        ↓
Governance, funding, and capacity
        ↓
Cross-functional execution
        ↓
Release readiness and adoption
        ↓
Benefits, metrics, and continuous improvement
```

My leadership principles are:

1. **Outcome over activity**: Measure success through business value, adoption, reliability, and customer impact.
2. **Transparency before escalation**: Surface risks early, clarify ownership, and present decision-ready options.
3. **Governance with purpose**: Keep controls proportionate, practical, and focused on better decisions.
4. **Technical curiosity**: Understand architecture and engineering trade-offs without replacing technical specialists.
5. **Predictable execution**: Combine realistic planning, dependency control, quality gates, and disciplined follow-through.
6. **People-centered leadership**: Create clarity, accountability, psychological safety, and space for teams to perform.
